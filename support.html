Common questions

When do my lines come back?
On the same date one year later (and every year after). While you’re starting out, Today also shows what you wrote a week and a month ago.

I wrote on February 29. When does it come back?
On February 28 in years without a leap day.

How do I move my journal to a new phone?
Your device backup includes it, or use Settings → Export all lines (CSV) and then Import lines from CSV on the new phone.

I bought Pro on another device.
Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple or Google account.

Can I import from another journal?
Yes — any CSV with a date column (yyyy-mm-dd or m/d/yyyy) and a text column. Days you’ve already written are never replaced.
