One line a day.
It comes back next year.

Write a single sentence about today. On the same date next year, Yearline shows it to you again — so the ordinary days become the ones you remember.

1 year ago
First cold morning. Wore the green scarf and walked to the river before work.
2 years ago
Moved into the new apartment. Ate dinner on boxes and it was perfect.

Small enough to keep

One sentence, a mood, and (with Pro) one photo. It takes less than a minute, which is why it lasts.

Private by design

No account. No cloud. No analytics. Your journal lives only on your phone.

What you get

  • “On this day” lookback — every earlier year, newest first, and a week or a month ago while you’re starting out
  • A calendar of your year, colored by mood
  • A gentle daily reminder that stays quiet once you’ve written
  • A home-screen and lock-screen widget with last year’s line
  • CSV export and import, free forever
  • Yearline Pro, one payment: a photo per day, your year as a PDF book, Face ID lock, a yearly recap card and extra themes

Available for iPhone and Android.
