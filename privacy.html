Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy covers Yearline: One Line a Day (“Yearline”, “the app”) on iPhone and Android. The short version: everything you write stays on your device. There is no account and the developer never receives your journal.

Your journal

Your lines, moods and photos are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to the developer or anyone else. The app has no server of its own. Your device’s own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Android Backup on Android) may include the app’s data if you have turned that on; that backup belongs to your Apple or Google account and the developer cannot read it.

Photos are only added when you choose one in the system photo picker; the app keeps a resized copy and never reads the rest of your library. Exports (CSV or PDF) go only where you send them from the share sheet.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification service or server is involved.

App Lock

Face ID, Touch ID or your device’s biometric/passcode check is handled by the operating system. The app only learns whether the check succeeded.

Purchases

Yearline Pro is a one-time in-app purchase through Apple’s App Store or Google Play, which process the payment under their own privacy policies; the developer never sees your payment details. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm the purchase and restore it; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and purchase receipt data, and nothing about your journal. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or profile.
  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs, and no ads.
  • No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a line in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: atabany.apps@gmail.com.
